About Buwan ng Wika

Buwan ng Wika is celebrated every August to promote the Filipino language, preserve our culture, and strengthen national identity.

Importance of the Filipino Language

  • Strengthens national identity.
  • Preserves Filipino culture and traditions.
  • Promotes unity among Filipinos.
  • Improves communication.
