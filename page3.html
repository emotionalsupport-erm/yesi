About Buwan ng Wika

Buwan ng Wika is celebrated every August to honor the Filipino language, culture, and traditions. It encourages every Filipino to appreciate and preserve our national language.

Importance of the Filipino Language

  • Promotes national identity.
  • Preserves Filipino culture.
  • Encourages unity among Filipinos.
  • Strengthens communication.
