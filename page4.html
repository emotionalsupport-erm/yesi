Within the walls of the imperial palace, secrets bloom like flowers. Among elegant courtyards, mysterious illnesses, and whispered rumors, curiosity can be more powerful than royalty.

Jinshi is a beautiful and influential eunuch who serves within the Imperial Palace. Known for his graceful appearance, charming personality, and sharp intelligence, he holds a powerful position despite his seemingly gentle nature. Jinshi often takes an interest in Maomao and frequently draws her into the palace's many mysteries. Beneath his elegant appearance lies a much more complicated identity and a deep connection to the affairs of the Imperial Palace.

🌸

Lishu

The youngest of the four concubines, Lishu is gentle and reserved. Despite her position in the Inner Palace, she struggles with loneliness and insecurity.

🪷

Lihua

Elegant and graceful, Lihua is one of the highest-ranking consorts. She is known for her beauty, dignity, and refined presence within the palace.

🌺

Gyokuyou

Intelligent, charismatic, and observant, Gyokuyou is one of the Emperor's favored consorts. Her calm confidence makes her a powerful presence in the Inner Palace.

🌿

Ah-Duo

Wise and composed, Ah-Duo is a former high-ranking consort respected for her strength and maturity. Her connection to the palace holds many fascinating secrets.

Maomao is a clever and highly observant young apothecary whose knowledge of medicine, herbs, and poisons makes her unique within the Imperial Palace. Originally working as an apothecary in the pleasure district, she is brought into the palace as a servant. Her curiosity and sharp mind soon lead her to investigate strange illnesses, unusual incidents, and hidden secrets. Although she often appears uninterested in palace politics, Maomao's intelligence, practical nature, and determination repeatedly place her at the center of important mysteries.
