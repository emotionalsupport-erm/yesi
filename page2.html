About Nutrition Month

Nutrition Month reminds everyone to eat healthy foods, stay active, and take care of their bodies. Good nutrition helps us grow strong and live a healthier life.

Healthy Eating Tips

🍎 Eat more fruits every day.
🥦 Include vegetables in every meal.
💧 Drink plenty of water.
🏃 Exercise regularly.
