Eat Right, Live Bright!

Celebrate Nutrition Month by choosing healthier foods, staying active, and encouraging everyone to build better eating habits. Good nutrition leads to a healthier and happier future.

Healthy Eating Tips

Eat More Vegetables

Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables every meal.

Choose Fruits

Fresh fruits are naturally sweet and packed with vitamins.

Drink Water

Stay hydrated by drinking at least 8 glasses of water daily.

Why Nutrition Matters

Good nutrition provides the energy we need to learn, play, and grow. Eating balanced meals rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and protein helps strengthen the immune system, improves concentration, and supports a healthy lifestyle. Making smart food choices today creates a brighter tomorrow.
