Celebrate Nutrition Month by choosing healthier foods, staying active, and encouraging everyone to build better eating habits.



Healthy Eating Tips

Eat More Vegetables

Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables every meal.

Choose Fruits

Fresh fruits are naturally sweet and packed with vitamins.

Drink Water

Drink at least 8 glasses of water every day.

Why Nutrition Matters

Good nutrition gives us energy to learn, play, and grow. Eating healthy foods helps our body stay strong and healthy.
